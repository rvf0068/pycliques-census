* Certified ten-v27 census update

A /complete/ is a nonempty pairwise adjacent vertex set and a /clique/ is an inclusion-maximal complete. We write \(K(G)\) for the maximal-clique intersection graph, \(P_0(G)\) for the complete dominated-vertex paring, and \(P_{j+1}(G)=P(K(P_j(G)))\). As usual \(O_d=\overline{dK_2}\).

The previous exact ten-v26 census retained \(1046\) indeterminate original ten-vertex graphs: \(430\) with nine-vertex pared cores and \(616\) with stiff ten-vertex cores. The full exact certificates from merged PR #103 prove that precisely two more original ten-vertex graphs are \(K\)-divergent. Ten-v27 therefore has \(1044=430+614\) indeterminate originals. The three unresolved original nine-vertex graphs are unchanged.

* Complete positive proof for both original McKay graphs

The permanent certificate =research/order10_five_p3_native_v1/positive_o11.json= and the independent replay module =verify_positive.py= establish a full ordinary retraction \(P_3(G_{11656793})\to O_{11}\). The third iterate has \(216\) vertices and \(19663\) edges. The proof checks all \(19663\) edges, the complete \(216\)-vertex projection, pointwise fixation of the target, and its eleven antipodal pairs. The historical exact global \(P_3\) class index provides a complete \(216\)-vertex isomorphism \(P_3(G_{11658674})\cong P_3(G_{11656793})\). The permanent verifier independently reconstructs the second original's \(P_3\) and checks all edges and nonedges under that bijection, thereby transporting the ordinary retraction to \(O_{11}\). No bounded-search timeout or candidate-specific obstruction is used as a classification certificate.

Because \(K(O_d)\cong O_{2^{d-1}}\) for \(d\ge3\) and ordinary retractions are preserved under clique iteration, both graphs are \(K\)-divergent. In fact both fourth pared clique iterates have a free \(\mathbb Z\) homology summand in dimension \(1023\) and at least \(2048\) vertices, by the general theorem proved in =research/order10_five_p3_native_v1/RESEARCH.org=. No explicit \(P_4\) is constructed.
