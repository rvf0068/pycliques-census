* Mathematically general finite-map consequences

A coaffine weak endomorphism of a finite graph \(X\) is a graph morphism \(f:X\to X\) satisfying \(f(v)\notin N_X[v]\) for every vertex. A graph morphism sends an adjacent pair to an adjacent pair or to the same vertex.

*Lemma 1 (eventual stable image).* Every coaffine weak endomorphism \(f:X\to X\) has an integer \(e\ge1\) such that \(r=f^e\) is an ordinary graph retraction onto the induced subgraph \(Y=X[S]\), where \(S\) is the eventual stable image of \(f\). The restriction \(f|_S\) is a coaffination of \(Y\).

*Proof.* The descending chain \(V(X)\supseteq f(V(X))\supseteq f^2(V(X))\supseteq\cdots\) stabilizes at \(S\). Then \(f(S)=S\), so \(f|_S\) is a permutation, and is an automorphism because its finite vertex-bijection preserves the edge set in both directions. Its coaffinity follows from the given vertex property. Choose a positive integer \(e\) at least the transient depth and divisible by the order of this permutation. Then \(f^e\) fixes every element of \(S\), maps all of \(V(X)\) into \(S\), and is a graph morphism. Thus \(r=f^e\) is an ordinary retraction. \(\square\)

*Lemma 2 (coaffine join decomposition).* Let \(Y\) admit a coaffination \(\alpha\), and let \(H=\overline Y\). The permutation \(\alpha\) acts on the connected components of \(H\). For each orbit of these components, take the union \(S_i\) of its vertices and let \(Y_i=Y[S_i]\). Then every \(Y_i\) is coaffine, and \(Y=Y_1*\cdots*Y_t\). In particular, if \(t\ge3\), \(Y\) is expansive by the Three Summands Theorem (Larrión--Neumann-Lara--Pizaña 2009, Theorem 3.6). If \(t\ge2\) and one \(Y_i\) is connected, \(Y\) is expansive by Neumann-Lara's Connected Summand Theorem (ibid., Theorem 4.6).

*Proof.* The automorphism of \(Y\) also preserves its complement, thus permutes complementary connected components. Each orbit union is invariant and inherits a coaffination, and distinct complementary components have no complement edges between them. Hence the claimed join. Apply the stated published theorems. \(\square\)

By the ordinary clique-retraction theorem, if this certificate appears for a third pared iterate, the original connected ten-vertex graph is \(K\)-divergent. The independent verifier checks every source edge and every condition in both lemmas, including the full graph automorphism on the eventual image. A positive native map alone, without a validated expansive join, is recorded as a positive retraction but is *not automatically classified divergent*.

* Resource-limited native experiment

We reuse the **existing public** =pycliques.find_coaffine_endomorphism= API, including its C++17 native backend and existing =label= and =fiber= value orderings. The solver checkout is the exact previously certified monorepo overhaul commit =e27649a6898b8c5b5bdbb4c6c64fd792672ae0ae=. The runtime dependencies and installed Python/C++ source Git blobs are pinned by the existing =research/order10_five_p3_native_v1/search.py= validator. There is no new CSP implementation, no change to =pycliques= or =pycombtop=, and no need to build the enormous fourth iterates.

Each original receives two independently budgeted tasks, one for each value ordering:

- At most 3000000 search states.
- At most 150 seconds for each native solver invocation.
- Only =FOUND=, =EXHAUSTED= or =LIMIT_REACHED= are accepted as true solver statuses.
- For =FOUND=, the complete source-to-source vertex map is stored and independently verified on **every edge**. The verifier computes the eventual stable image, exact idempotent power, all target edges, component-orbit coaffine join factors, and the exact applicable published divergence theorem.
- A limited search is inconclusive. Even an exhaustive failure to find a coaffine weak endomorphism would **not** prove clique convergence; it would exclude only this particular divergence mechanism at this stage.

The six complete individual outcomes and one independent aggregate audit are saved as separate workflow artifacts. Only a discovered positive map accompanied by a complete, permanent, independent theorem certificate can justify a later census promotion.
