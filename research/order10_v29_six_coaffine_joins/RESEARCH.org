* The setting

Let \(K(X)\) be the graph whose vertices are the inclusion-maximal nonempty complete subgraphs of \(X\), adjacent when they intersect. Define the complete ordinary dominated-vertex paring \(P\) and \(P_0(G)=P(G)\), \(P_{n+1}(G)=P(K(P_n(G)))\). We use \(O_d=\overline{dK_2}\) for the octahedral graph, and \(*\) for graph join.

The frozen connected-ten index =indeterminate_order_10_v28.txt= (Git blob =35bb83b56bb8b4fd453b0ba272d354dc6b42e68c=) had 1043 unresolved original McKay indices. Of these, 430 had unresolved nine-vertex pared cores and 613 had stiff ten-vertex pared cores.

Two complete positive proof archives recently merged as GitHub PRs #108 and #109 certify six *stiff* ten-vertex originals as \(K\)-divergent. PR #110 contains supplementary independent native searches (including 3066 seeded coaffine trials and a complete 511-class octahedral pilot), but its resource-limited or exhausted statuses do not justify any further census change.

*Theorem.* All six original connected ten-vertex graphs listed above are \(K\)-divergent.

*Proof.* The four \(P_3\) certificates independently reconstruct their complete third pared clique iterates from the original ten-vertex graph6, check every original edge under the complete archived coaffine weak endomorphism, compute the exact eventual image, and check that the indicated tenth or fourteenth power is an ordinary graph retraction, fixing every image vertex pointwise. Each stable target is explicitly verified as the displayed graph join, with a valid coaffination on every factor and all required cross-edges. The two \(P_1\) certificates independently reconstruct each complete first pared clique graph with NetworkX and bitset Bron--Kerbosch maximal-clique enumerators and complete paring, checking exactly the same factor and cross-edge conditions.

The *Connected Summand Theorem* (Theorem 4.6) and *Three Summands Theorem* (Theorem 3.6) of F. Larrión, V. Neumann-Lara and M. A. Pizaña, /On expansive graphs/, /European Journal of Combinatorics/ *30* (2009), 372--379, DOI =10.1016/j.ejc.2008.05.005=, imply that each certified join is expansive and therefore \(K\)-divergent. Ordinary graph retractions transfer divergence from the target to the source, and complete dominated-vertex paring preserves eventual clique-graph behavior. Thus the original six ten-vertex graphs are divergent. \(\square\)
