* Research method and finite proof discipline

We use the existing public =pycliques.find_octahedral_retraction_coaffine= function, not a new graph-specific solver, at the exact merged monorepo PR #56 commit =e27649a6898b8c5b5bdbb4c6c64fd792672ae0ae=. Each of the five authenticated \(P_3\) graphs is tested with both =value_order="label"= and =value_order="fiber"=, each with exactly \(2{,}000{,}000\) search states and \(90\) seconds. The native C++ solver is built explicitly with the existing monorepo command. The exact runtime versions are pinned to the distributions recorded in the overhaul's =uv.lock= using the committed =constraints.txt=. The pilot additionally checks immutable Git blob hashes of seven installed Python and C++ solver files and records all resolved package versions in every search artifact. This verifies installed source code, not merely an environment variable claiming a revision. Different value orderings may find different positive maps; neither changes the soundness of a checked certificate.

A =FOUND= result must supply complete antipodal pairs and an ordinary graph retraction onto an induced \(O_d=\overline{dK_2}\), with \(d\ge3\). The independent census verifier checks the nonedges within antipodal pairs, all cross-pair edges, fixation of every target vertex, and the entire source edge set; only such a positive certificate establishes \(K\)-divergence. =EXHAUSTED= is a genuine exhaustive negative conclusion about this particular target family at depth three, not about \(K\)-behavior in general. =LIMIT_REACHED= is inconclusive and must never be interpreted as absence of a retraction.

* General homological and size consequences of an octahedral retraction

** Flag vertex lower bound

*Lemma.* Let \(X\) be a graph whose clique complex has nonzero reduced homology \(\widetilde H_k(\Delta(X);\mathbb F)\) over a field \(\mathbb F\), with \(k\ge0\). Then \(X\) has at least \(2k+2\) vertices.

*Proof.* Pass to an induced subgraph \(Y\subseteq X\) with nonzero \(\widetilde H_k(\Delta(Y);\mathbb F)\) and minimal possible vertex set. For \(k=0\), nonzero reduced \(H_0\) requires at least two vertices. Suppose \(k\ge1\), choose \(v\in V(Y)\), and write \(L=\Delta(Y[N_Y(v)])\) for its link. By minimality, \(\widetilde H_k(\Delta(Y-v);\mathbb F)=0\). The deletion--link long exact sequence and the decomposition \(\Delta(Y)=\Delta(Y-v)\cup(v*L)\) give an injection from nonzero \(\widetilde H_k(\Delta(Y);\mathbb F)\) into \(\widetilde H_{k-1}(L;\mathbb F)\), so the latter is nonzero. By induction, \(|N_Y(v)|\ge2k\). Since \(v\) cannot be universal (otherwise \(\Delta(Y)\) would be a cone), there is at least one nonneighbor of \(v\) outside the link. Hence \(|V(Y)|\ge1+2k+1=2k+2\), and the same bound holds for \(X\). \(\square\)

** Theorem for next pared clique iterates

*Theorem.* If \(P_j(G)\) admits an ordinary graph retraction onto \(O_d=\overline{dK_2}\), with \(d\ge3\), then the reduced integral homology of \(\Delta(P_{j+1}(G))\) has \(\mathbb Z\) as a direct summand in dimension \(2^{d-1}-1\), and \(|V(P_{j+1}(G))|\ge2^d\).

*Proof.* The clique-graph functor preserves ordinary graph retractions. Applying it to \(P_j(G)\to O_d\) gives an ordinary graph retraction \(K(P_j(G))\to K(O_d)\cong O_{2^{d-1}}\). The clique complex of \(O_m\) is the join of \(m\) copies of \(S^0\), hence the sphere \(S^{m-1}\). Its integral homology injects as a direct summand under the retraction. The complete dominated-vertex paring \(K(P_j(G))\to P_{j+1}(G)\) is a homotopy equivalence of clique complexes, so the same nonzero free homology summand survives. The preceding flag vertex lemma, with \(k=2^{d-1}-1\), gives \(|V(P_{j+1}(G))|\ge2(2^{d-1}-1)+2=2^d\). \(\square\)

For both original ten-vertex graphs McKay 11656793 and 11658674 the certified (P_3)-isomorphism and (O_{11}) retraction give (d=11), so (\widetilde H_{1023}(\Delta(P_4(G));\mathbb Z)) has a direct (\mathbb Z)-summand and (|V(P_4(G))|\ge2048) without enumerating their enormous fourth iterates. The previously certified \(P_3\to O_{12}, O_{14}, O_{15}\) targets likewise force lower bounds of \(4096\), \(16384\), and \(32768\) vertices respectively in their fourth pared clique iterates. These are *positive homological* consequences and are not inferred from an enumeration timeout.

#+begin_src jupyter-python
import networkx as nx
from research.order10_overcap_p3_coaffine_v1.pilot import source_catalog
from research.order10_five_p3_native_v1.search import REMAINING
hosts = source_catalog()
for original_index in REMAINING:
    third = nx.from_graph6_bytes(hosts[original_index]["p3_graph6"].encode("ascii"))
    complement = nx.complement(third)
    triangles = sum(nx.triangles(complement).values()) // 3
    print(original_index, (len(third), third.number_of_edges()), (complement.number_of_edges(), triangles))
#+end_src
