* Scope and definitions

A /complete/ is a nonempty set of pairwise adjacent vertices. A /clique/ is an inclusion-maximal complete. Write \(K(G)\) for the intersection graph of cliques, \(P_0(G)\) for a complete dominated-vertex paring, and \(P_{j+1}(G)=P(K(P_j(G)))\). * Complete positive theorem

Let \(G\) be original connected McKay 11713984 and \(X=P_3(G)\). The source \(X\) has 169 vertices and 12897 edges. The permanent archive =research/order10_11713984_coaffine_retract_v1/certificate.json= supplies a complete coaffine weak endomorphism \(f:X\to X\) and pins its bytes. An independent checker rebuilds the original graph, verifies \(f(v)\notin N_X[v]\) for every vertex and verifies the graph-morphism property on every edge.

Its image \(S=f(V(X))\) has 35 vertices. The restriction \(f|_S\) is a permutation consisting of fourteen 2-cycles and one 7-cycle; therefore \(r=f^{14}\) fixes the whole image and is an *ordinary graph retraction* \(X\to Y=X[S]\). The verifier also checks all 12897 source edges under \(r\). The complementary graph of \(Y\) is exactly fourteen disjoint edges together with a seven-cycle. Thus

\[
\boxed{Y\cong O_{14}*\overline{C_7}.}
\]

*Theorem (positive classification).* The original graph \(G\) is \(K\)-divergent.

*Proof.* The octahedral summand \(O_{14}\) admits the automorphism interchanging the endpoints of each of its fourteen missing edges. The graph \(\overline{C_7}\) is connected and has a coaffination given by a one-step cyclic rotation; independently, the verifier checks the restriction of the archived \(f\) to its seven vertices on every ordered vertex pair. Thus the two summands are coaffine and one is connected. By Neumann-Lara's Connected Summand Theorem, *Theorem 4.6* of F. Larrión, V. Neumann-Lara and M. A. Pizaña, /On expansive graphs/, /European Journal of Combinatorics/ *30* (2009), 372--379, DOI =10.1016/j.ejc.2008.05.005=, their Zykov sum \(Y\) is expansive, hence \(K\)-divergent. Ordinary graph retractions preserve \(K\)-divergence from target to source; consequently \(X\) is \(K\)-divergent. Finally dominated-vertex paring preserves \(K\)-behavior, so the original \(G\) is \(K\)-divergent. \(\square\)

The positive witness is an ordinary retraction to *the entire 35-vertex join*, not a hypothetical retraction to \(O_{14}\) alone. Neither a limited native search nor an extremely large finite iterate is used to deduce divergence.
