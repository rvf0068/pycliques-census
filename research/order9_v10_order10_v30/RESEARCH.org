* The three final nine-originals

We write \(K(G)\) for the intersection graph of the inclusion-maximal
nonempty complete subgraphs (cliques) of \(G\). Let \(P(G)\) denote
complete closed-neighborhood dominated-vertex paring; write
\(P_0(G)=P(G)\) and \(P_{n+1}(G)=P(K(P_n(G)))\).
As usual, \(O_d=\overline{dK_2}\) is the octahedral graph on \(2d\)
vertices. The vertex labels below are original connected unlabeled
McKay indices, not indices in a collection of isomorphism types.

*Theorem 1.* The connected nine-vertex originals 249576, 251628,
260040 are \(K\)-divergent.

*Proof.* Starting with the respective original nine-vertex graph6
strings, two independent maximal-clique algorithms agree at each
fourth-iterate construction step: NetworkX maximal-clique enumeration
and bitset Bron--Kerbosch. Each complete closed-neighborhood paring
is verified by checking every claimed neighborhood inclusion,
replaying the successive fold moves, and checking that the final
graph is stiff.

For the three nine-originals, the resulting fourth completely pared
clique iterates are as follows.

| Nine-original | Original graph6 | \(P_3(G)\) order / edges | \(P_4(G)\) order / edges | Ordinary induced retract |
|-
| 249576 | =HEhfvzu= | 53 / 1044 | 685 / 199283 | \(O_{17}\) |
| 251628 | =HEhvVn{= | 55 / 1156 | 1034 / 467517 | \(O_{20}\) |
| 260040 | =HQyvex}= | 58 / 1289 | 1227 / 658528 | \(O_{22}\) |

Every induced antipodal pair, every cross-edge between different
pairs, every source-to-target vertex image, and each of the
1,325,328 original \(P_4\) edges are independently checked.
The saved maps fix their respective octahedral targets pointwise,
and thus are ordinary graph retractions, not merely homomorphisms.

The classical exact identity \(K(O_d)\cong O_{2^{d-1}}\) (for \(d\ge2\))
implies that each \(O_d\) with \(d\ge3\) is \(K\)-divergent. The
Neumann-Lara clique-divergence retraction theorem and the
dominated-vertex paring theorem transfer \(K\)-divergence to
the original nine-vertex graph. \(\square\)

*Theorem 2.* These 500 connected ten-vertex originals are
\(K\)-divergent.

*Proof.* For each source \(X\), a completely pared iterate
\(P_j(X)\) is explicitly isomorphic to a completely pared iterate
of one of the nine-vertex graphs of Theorem 1, or (in the
original pared-nine cases) \(P_0(X)\) itself is isomorphic
to the indicated nine-vertex graph. The isomorphism is certified
either on all graph pairs at P3/P4 or directly on the original
nine-vertex core. The corresponding further iterates therefore
coincide up to isomorphism. Complete dominated-vertex paring
preserves eventual clique behavior, and all three target nine-vertex
originals have just been proved \(K\)-divergent. \(\square\)
